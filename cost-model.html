Cost model & rental pricing · indicative Class 4 estimate

What it costs to build, and what to charge for it

A bottom-up model of the 25,000 bopd Ad Daimah rental EPF: 40 capital lines, 13 operating lines, and a monthly operating rate built from capital recovery, operating cost, risk and margin. Change any assumption and the tariff, the module rates and the investor return recompute.

Adjust the assumptions See the bid rate card

Not a Company-approved cost estimate. Every number below is the Contractor’s own Class 4 estimate (±25%) built from the RFQ documents and 2026 Gulf rental-market unit rates. It is a pricing tool, not a quotation, and it excludes Company scope.

01 — Headline

The number to bid, and the floor under it

The monthly rate is fully inclusive — equipment, people, consumables, maintenance and support — because the Scope of Work deems everything required to run the facility to be included. Price per barrel is derived, not charged: the rent is monthly.

Where the monthly rate goes

Recommended bid, split by driver

Capital recovery is the largest block because the firm term is short relative to the asset life: a 36-month term has to return the whole capital bucket. Lengthening the term is the single most effective way to cut the tariff — see the sensitivity section.

04 — Operating cost

OPEX: what 95% availability actually costs

The availability guarantee is bought with people, spares and maintenance. Rotational staffing in southern Iraq, three separate operator teams, a laboratory per train and a full spares holding are all in here — and all of it is at the Contractor’s expense.

What dominates

People are the largest single line — roughly two fifths of OPEX. That is a fixed cost: it does not fall when throughput falls, which is why the cost per barrel rises sharply at low utilisation and why the 20% turndown case matters commercially.

What the Company supplies

Produced gas for power generation (Basis of Design: Phase 1 gas is consumed to generate electricity), civil works and foundations, flowlines, wellsite facilities and the export pipeline system. Diesel is modelled only as backup.

05 — Price build-up and return

Cost, risk and margin converted into a rate

The capital bucket is annuitised over the firm term at the weighted average cost of capital, operating cost is added at its modelled level, a risk allowance covers availability penalties and feed variability, and the target margin is applied to the price.

Why the IRR sits above the cost of capital

The capital charge already returns the weighted average cost of capital — the annuity fully repays the capital bucket and pays 11% on it. The risk allowance and the target margin are the Contractor’s reward on top, so the IRR lands well above WACC. That gap is the room you have to sharpen the price without going below cost.

Read the two numbers together: the required monthly rate is the floor that recovers capital and OPEX with nothing for the Contractor; the recommended bid is the floor plus risk and margin. Anything you bid between them is commercially defensible, and the sensitivity table shows what each concession costs you in return.

Term beats price

A 36-month term amortises the entire capital bucket over three years. Extending to 48 or 60 months is worth more to the tariff than a 15% procurement saving, and costs the Company nothing if the facilities would be rented anyway.

Availability is a revenue line

Two points below the guarantee costs 6% of the monthly rate. At this scale that is more than the entire maintenance budget difference between a good and a poor maintenance strategy — which is why the availability case is modelled explicitly, not assumed away.

Estimate quality

Class 4 (AACE) — prepared from concept flowsheets and equipment counts, not from quotations. Accuracy −15% / +25%. All figures are real 2026 US dollars, no escalation; add the Company’s escalation basis if the bid is indexed. Tax is modelled at the input rate on revenue less OPEX less straight-line depreciation; financing structure, withholding tax and currency exposure are not modelled.
